# Open Future Forum

Open Future Forum is a global executive community and publisher of open research, datasets, market maps and practical resources for executive leaders.

This GitHub organization is the open evidence and source layer for those resources. Public reading experiences, canonical publications and source repositories are linked separately so their roles remain clear.

## Start here

- [Open Future Forum research and resource directory](https://openfutureforum.github.io/) — the public entry point to the organization’s open knowledge projects.
- [OpenFutureForum.com](https://openfutureforum.com/) — the official site for the executive community, events, programs and canonical OFF research publications.

## Open resources

- [CXO Ecosystem Index](https://openfutureforum.github.io/cxo-ecosystem-index/) — a structured view of companies, communities, advisors and resources serving leaders across the C-suite. [Source repository](https://github.com/OpenFutureForum/cxo-ecosystem-index).
- [Executive Communities Index](https://openfutureforum.github.io/executive-communities-index/) — structured data and research on executive communities, CEO peer groups and C-suite networks. [Source repository](https://github.com/OpenFutureForum/executive-communities-index).
- [Executive Community Playbook](https://openfutureforum.github.io/executive-community-playbook/) — practical guidance and reusable templates for designing and operating executive communities. [Source repository](https://github.com/OpenFutureForum/executive-community-playbook).
- [Executive AI Research](https://openfutureforum.github.io/executive-ai-research/) — a public reading layer for OFF benchmark reports, market maps, datasets and selected industry research. Original OFF reports remain canonical at [openfutureforum.com/research](https://openfutureforum.com/research/). [Source repository](https://github.com/OpenFutureForum/executive-ai-research).
- [Executive Intelligence Index](https://github.com/OpenFutureForum/executive-intelligence-index) — a cited executive-intelligence research framework. The repository is the current public project location; staging work is not presented as published evidence.

## Publishing approach

- **Readable:** substantive projects provide a human-facing public site when the material is ready for publication.
- **Traceable:** repositories expose methods, source trails, structured data, release history and correction paths where available.
- **Scoped:** OFF-owned material, third-party evidence, limitations and canonical sources are identified explicitly.
- **Correctable:** public issues and pull requests provide a visible path for review and improvement.
